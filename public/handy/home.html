Common questions

Everything you might be wondering.

How much does Danny charge?
$75/hour with a one-hour minimum for standard-rate work (weekdays, daytime). After-hours (nights, weekends, holidays) is $131/hour. Estimates are always free — text a photo of what you need done, you'll get a fixed price before anyone rolls out.
Do you serve mobile-home parks and HOAs?
Yes — this is Danny's specialty. He knows single-wide layouts, park skirting rules, and HOA paint-approval processes across Tucson, Oro Valley, Sahuarita, Marana, and Green Valley. If your park or community has a preferred-vendor list, ask about getting Danny added.
Are you insured?
Fully covered by general liability insurance. Certificate of insurance available on request — some HOAs need it on file before work starts. Just ask.
Do you offer a warranty?
Every job comes with a 90-day workmanship warranty. If something Danny installed or repaired fails within 90 days from workmanship (not from a separate cause), he'll come back and fix it at no charge.
What jobs are too big for a handyman?
Under Arizona ROC handyman exemption A.R.S. § 32-1121(A)(14), Danny works on jobs under $1,000 (materials + labor combined) that don't require a specialty contractor license. For bigger jobs (whole-home rewires, HVAC installs, roof replacements, structural work), he'll refer you to a licensed contractor he trusts.
What if my job runs longer than the estimate?
Danny gives you a fixed price before starting, based on his estimate of the job. If he hits something unexpected mid-job, he pauses and tells you — you decide whether to keep going (with a revised quote) or stop there. No surprise bills.
How do I pay?
You'll get a digital invoice with a payment link. Card, ACH, or Venmo/Zelle all accepted. Pay when the work is done — no deposits required for most jobs under $500.
How fast can Danny get to me?
Same-week is the norm. Emergencies (leaks, lockouts, no-AC-in-July) get after-hours slots at $131/hour. Danny's a solo operator with a real calendar — you'll always see what's actually available.
